    4 If it already happened

    Act in the first hour. Money can still be frozen.

    Being scammed is not your fault — these scripts are designed by professionals to fool anyone. What matters now is speed.

    1. Stop everything.

      Don’t pay more, don’t click anything else, don’t share any more details. If they’re still on the call, hang up.

    2. Call 1930 immediately.

      India’s National Cybercrime Helpline. They can ask banks to freeze the money trail — the sooner, the better the chance.

    3. Call your bank.

      Ask for the fraud desk. Freeze the card / account, dispute the transaction, and get a complaint number.

    4. File a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in

      Keep the transaction ID, screenshots and the scammer’s number or UPI ID ready. You can file anonymously too.

    5. Save the evidence.

      Screenshots of chats, call logs, SMS, payment receipts. Don’t delete anything — it helps the investigation.

    6. Change your passwords.

      If you shared an OTP, PIN or password, change it everywhere you reuse it, and turn on two-factor authentication.
